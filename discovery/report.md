# Conditional keys discovered on Hockey, VOC, NCVoter and UniProt

Generated by `discovery/run.py`, `discovery/run_csv.py` and `discovery/report.py`. The Hockey and VOC exports are pseudonymised, so their constants are shown by frequency rank within the column (`lgID = v1 (63%)` is the most frequent league; the JSON keeps the hashed value). NCVoter and UniProt are plaintext. NCVoter was mined on all 1,023,993 records with curated key columns (person attributes) and condition columns (status, reason, party, demographics, flags); keys mined on a 100,000-row sample did not hold on the full data and are superseded. The UniProt file holds 24,173 complete records (it is truncated relative to its name), all of which were mined. Possible keys are ranked by the share of in-scope records that have all key values (SQL UNIQUE ignores the others), and keys on columns that are more than 80% empty are treated as accidental. Every listed exact key was re-checked on the data by an independent test (column *ok*).
